# 🌐 Global Sales & Profit Intelligence Suite

## 🎯 The Challenge
Leadership was losing valuable hours every single week manually reconciling over 10,000 global transactions across scattered regional spreadsheets. This lack of a unified, automated view created severe operational friction:

* **The Reporting Bottleneck:** Financial teams had to manually stitch together disparate regional datasets, stalling executive visibility into performance metrics.
* **The Margin Blind Spot:** Without a clean, centralized system, isolating true **Net Profit** from raw **Gross Sales** was nearly impossible, leaving leadership blind to actual product line margins.
* **Delayed Decision-Making:** Compiling fiscal-year reports took so long that by the time stakeholders received the data, it was already outdated, preventing rapid strategic pivots.

---

## 💡 The Solution
I engineered a centralized, automated data engine and high-density executive dashboard using advanced Power Query ETL pipelines and relational data modeling.

### ⚙️ Automated Data Transformation Pipeline
* **Self-Maintaining ETL:** Built a robust **Power Query** pipeline that automatically ingests, cleans, and standardizes messy raw sales data across global regions instantly upon refresh.
* **Silo Elimination:** Replaced fragile manual data tracking with a single, structural data ingestion layer, enforcing strict schema compliance across all transaction inputs.

### 📊 Dynamic Profit Monitoring Engine
* **Source-to-Visual Optimization:** Created centralized transactional models that handle heavy currency and revenue math natively, keeping frontend reporting fast and highly responsive.
* **Multi-Dimensional Pivoting:** Developed real-time analytical layers allowing leadership to instantly slice, dice, and filter financial metrics across regions, product categories, or fiscal timelines without needing to rebuild or recalculate underlying tables.

---

## 📈 The Impact

* ⚡ **Executive Decision Velocity:** Slashed the time required for monthly and quarterly financial reviews **from hours of frustrating manual work down to a split-second, automated dashboard refresh**.
* 🎯 **Eliminated Operational Blind Spots:** Provided immediate clarity on high-margin regions versus bleeding or underperforming product lines, empowering leadership to make data-backed, tactical business maneuvers in real time.
* 🔒 **100% Data Integrity:** Replaced error-prone, disconnected spreadsheets with a single, locked-down **Source of Truth**, ensuring that every financial calculation is mathematically verified, fully reconciled, and audit-ready.
